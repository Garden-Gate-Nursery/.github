# Welcome to Garden Gate Nursery 🌲

## Our Story

Garden Gate Nursery was founded in 2005 by hay farmers with a vision to bring quality container trees to the Pacific Northwest. What started with a couple hundred container trees has grown into a thriving nursery serving the Pacific Northwest and beyond.

Today, we operate two growing locations and provide thousands of container trees each year to meet the diverse needs of our customers.

## What We Do

We specialize in container-grown trees, offering a wide selection of species suitable for the Pacific Northwest climate and beyond. Our trees are carefully cultivated at our two growing locations to ensure the highest quality.
